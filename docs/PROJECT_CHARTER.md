# Project Charter - BusinessOS

## Owner objective

Give an AI an isolated computer, an economic objective, an explicit budget, and bounded authorizations. The AI should autonomously research opportunities, develop tools, delegate scoped work, verify deliverables and adjust strategy based on independently grounded facts. A human owner should not need to understand each trade or application to evaluate reliable evidence.

## Scope

- Multi-industry; do not pre-select freelancing, ecommerce, media, or affiliate sales.
- Model-independent architecture with replaceable research, execution, verification and accounting adapters.
- Maintain evidence and accountability for every experiment.
- Fail closed for external money movement, identity, regulated professional advice, secret access and policy modification.
- Host public code on GitHub, private operational data elsewhere.

## Success metrics by gate

1. V0.1: offline runnable project, tests, immutable-ish local event history, explicit permission decisions.
2. V0.2: isolated computer tool executor; bounded agent delegation, restart/resume; independent deterministic test suite.
3. V0.3: authenticated public research with source evidence and provenance, not made-up market signals.
4. V0.4: authorized external pilot with a clear partner/channel, approval queue and reconciled receipts.
5. V1.0: robust long-running service with recoverable tasks, kill switch, financial reconciliation and measurable conversion/cost data.

## Out of scope for engineering milestone

- No trading speculation guarantees.
- No fabricated identities or consumer reviews.
- No unsolicited bulk platform messaging.
- No unlicensed regulated individual legal, tax or medical advice.
- No misuse of GitHub Actions as a free 24/7 server.

## Governance

Project owner authorizes public code. Real customer data and paid third-party accounts require separate approval and documented service terms. All external effects must be attributable to a request and approval record.
